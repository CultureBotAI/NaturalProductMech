A producer is not an occurrence. This corpus keeps the two apart because most sources do not. {{ stats.producer_claims }} producer claims say a taxon makes the compound, and only {{ stats.causal }} of those rest on evidence that addressed the organism. {{ stats.occurrence_records }} records carry a cited occurrence, which says only that someone found the compound there. Nothing promotes one to the other.
