Step 1: Decarboxylation of L-lysine to cadaverine and FAD-dependent N-hydroxylation to N-hydroxycadaverine in the Streptomyces desABCD pathway.

Step 2: Acylation of N-hydroxycadaverine with acetyl-CoA and succinyl-CoA followed by ATP-dependent trimerization and terminal N-acetylation to yield desferrioxamine D1 (N-acetyl-desferrioxamine B).
