Biosynthesis curated

Written by a curator with claim-level evidence on each step; not derived from a source. This is what the rest of the record exists to support.

StepEnzymeReactionSubstrate → productEvidence
1 DesA (lysine decarboxylase) CHEBI:18019 → CHEBI:18127 PMID:15600304
2 DesB (cadaverine monooxygenase) and DesC (N-hydroxycadaverine acyltransferase) CHEBI:18127 + CHEBI:15380 → ungrounded PMID:15600304
3 DesD (NRPS-independent siderophore synthetase) CHEBI:15380 → CHEBI:50439 PMID:17704773

Step 1: Pyridoxal 5'-phosphate-dependent decarboxylation of L-lysine to cadaverine; first committed step of the desABCD desferrioxamine biosynthetic pathway in Streptomyces coelicolor A3(2).

Step 2: FAD-dependent N-hydroxylation of cadaverine to N-hydroxycadaverine followed by DesC-catalyzed N-succinylation with succinyl-CoA to form N-hydroxy-N-succinylcadaverine (HSC) monomers.

Step 3: ATP-dependent iterative condensation of three N-hydroxy-N-succinylcadaverine units without macrocyclization to yield linear desferrioxamine G.

Mechanism & Biosynthesis Overview

Causal graph — bioactivity 11 nodes, 10 edges

DesABCD-mediated biosynthesis of linear tris-hydroxamate siderophore desferrioxamine G from L-lysine and succinyl-CoA and subsequent chelation of physiological Fe(III) as well as critical minerals Ga(III), Co(II), Ni(II), and La(III).
