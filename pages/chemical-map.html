Method and provenance

Model morgan-count-chiral-r2_0.90-r4_0.10+tanimoto+umap-precomputed-n15-d0.05-c2-rs42. The generator parses stored SMILES first and falls back to standard InChI when RDKit rejects the stored SMILES. This build used that fallback for 0 records and retained 19 multi-fragment records. Of 129 detected stereoisomer pairs, 128 have nonzero model distance. The remaining 1 sit at distance zero and therefore on one point: a Morgan fingerprint encodes tetrahedral chirality through its atom invariants and does not encode sulfoxide stereochemistry, so sulfoxide epimers hash identically at every radius. Those records are distinct and correct — their InChIKeys differ — and it is this map's distance metric that cannot separate them.

Distance is 90% radius-2 and 10% radius-4 chiral Morgan count-fingerprint Tanimoto distance. Coordinates are generated with UMAP(metric=precomputed, n_neighbors=15, min_dist=0.05, random_state=42). A corpus or model-version change can move every point.
