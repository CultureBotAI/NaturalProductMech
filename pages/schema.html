LinkML schema for individual natural product chemical structures.

One NaturalProductRecord is ONE chemical structure — a compound with a defined connectivity (Standard InChIKey), not a compound class and not a preparation. "Indole alkaloid" is a classification and appears as `np_pathway` / `compound_classes` on the records it covers; strychnine is a record. An extract, fraction, essential oil or herbal preparation is never a record: activity measured on a mixture is not evidence about any constituent of it.

A compound is admitted by an ASSERTED ORIGIN — a biosynthetic gene cluster, a cited occurrence in a taxon, or an allow-listed ChEBI specialized-metabolite role — never by a computed natural-product likeness or a structural resemblance to a known class.

The distinction this schema exists to keep is between `producer_organisms` (this taxon biosynthesizes the compound, with biosynthesis-grade evidence) and `occurrences` (this compound was detected in or isolated from this taxon, with a citation). A compound isolated from a sponge may be made by its bacterial symbiont. The seeder never promotes one field to the other.

Records are harmonized from MIBiG (producers, gene clusters, cluster class), ChEBI (identity, structures, specialized-metabolite roles), LOTUS via Wikidata (cited occurrences) and PubChem (structures), with the filing classification computed by NPClassifier. See docs/HARMONIZATION.md.

Follows the curation pattern of AntibioticMech, TraitMech, CultureMech, MediaIngredientMech, CommunityMech, HabitatMech, CellStructureMech and ProteinTraitsMech: one YAML per entity, ontology-grounded, evidence-backed, closed-schema validated, reproducible from committed inputs.

NaturalProductRecord

One natural product chemical structure, keyed by its Standard InChIKey and grounded in a ChEBI CURIE where one exists. Carries identity, structure, who makes it, where it has been found, how it is made, what it does, and the evidence for each.

ProducerEvidenceBasisEnum

What supports the claim that a TAXON makes a compound. Every value names evidence gathered IN THE ORGANISM — that is what makes the set coherent, and why heterologous expression is not among them: it establishes what a cloned locus can do elsewhere, which is `ClusterLinkEvidenceEnum`'s question, not this one.

BGC_CHARACTERIZED
A gene cluster shown to be sufficient or necessary — heterologous expression, knock-out studies, enzymatic assays, in-vitro expression.
BGC_CORRELATED
Expression and production correlate, or genomic and metabolomic data correlate. A real producer claim, but equally consistent with co-regulation, a neighbouring cluster, or a shared precursor. Never silently upgraded to BGC_CHARACTERIZED.
ISOTOPE_FEEDING
No description.
AXENIC_CULTURE
Production by an axenic culture of the taxon.
SOURCE_ASSERTION
A curated database asserts production, citing a report nobody here has read. The weakest basis that still counts as a producer claim, and the commonest: it covers a MIBiG entry whose structured evidence was never migrated from the legacy format, and every entry whose evidence establishes only what the cloned locus does. In both cases the taxon-makes-compound claim rests on the cited isolation literature, which is the right kind of evidence for it.

ClusterLinkEvidenceEnum

How well supported is the claim that a particular locus produces a compound. Grades the cluster, not the organism.

CLUSTER_DEMONSTRATED
Shown experimentally — heterologous expression, knock-out, enzymatic or in-vitro assay. The locus is sufficient or necessary.
CLUSTER_CORRELATED
Expression or abundance correlates with production. Consistent with this locus being responsible, and with a neighbouring one.
CLUSTER_PREDICTED
Inferred from sequence similarity. Not a demonstration.
CLUSTER_UNSTATED
The source records the locus and the compound but states no evidence for the link. In MIBiG 4.0 this is the legacy-format backlog, 1,627 of 2,437 active entries, and it means the annotation is missing rather than that the link is doubted.
