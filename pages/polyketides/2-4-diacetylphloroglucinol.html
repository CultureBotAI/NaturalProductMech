Step 1: Q2-87 PhlD is represented as the upstream type III polyketide synthase required for MAPG and 2,4-DAPG formation. The exact malonyl-CoA to phloroglucinol reaction is biochemically supported for the Pf-5 PhlD ortholog and marked proposed here because Bangera and Thomashow genetically placed Q2-87 PhlD upstream of MAPG before the PhlD reaction was biochemically revised.

Step 2: The step is kept as a multi-protein Q2-87 module because the 1999 paper showed PhlA, PhlC, and PhlB are collectively needed to convert MAPG to 2,4-DAPG without assigning separable individual reactions.
