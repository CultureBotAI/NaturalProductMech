What these status tags mean

Curation status describes a workflow state. SEEDED means the record entered through the source-seeding workflow; it does not imply that no later curation occurred. PROPOSED marks an addition awaiting review; REVIEWED records curator sign-off on identity, structure, filing class and producer claims; DEPRECATED retains a superseded or out-of-scope record. These are workflow decisions, not independently verified human review. Agent-assisted contributions may appear in the record history.

Grounding describes the identifier mapping: EXACT uses an ontology identity asserted by the source; MINTED keeps a local identifier when no defensible ontology identity is available; REVIEW_NEEDED flags an exact structure whose ontology identity needs adjudication. The grounding note retains any unresolved alternatives. These tags do not establish the strength of producer or activity evidence.

Step 1: GsfA is proposed to form the heptaketide benzophenone from acetyl-CoA and six malonyl-CoA units; Rhea captures this non-reducing polyketide synthase reaction as RHEA:73915.

Step 2: GsfB methylates the GsfA benzophenone product at 3-OH to yield griseophenone D, the immediate substrate for GsfC; Rhea captures the SAM-dependent methyltransferase reaction as RHEA:73923.

Step 3: GsfC methylates griseophenone D at 9-OH to yield griseophenone C, the immediate substrate for GsfI; Rhea captures the SAM-dependent methyltransferase reaction as RHEA:73927.

Step 4: GsfI chlorinates griseophenone C at C13 to form griseophenone B immediately upstream of GsfF-catalyzed grisan scaffold formation; Rhea captures the reduced-flavin chlorination as RHEA:73931.

Step 5: GsfF performs the late oxidative phenolic coupling that converts griseophenone B into the grisan scaffold of desmethyl-dehydrogriseofulvin during griseofulvin biosynthesis.

Step 6: GsfD is proposed to methylate desmethyl-dehydrogriseofulvin at 5-OH to yield dehydrogriseofulvin; Rhea maps the SAM-dependent GsfD O-methyltransferase to RHEA:73939.

Step 7: GsfE stereospecifically reduces dehydrogriseofulvin to griseofulvin with NADPH; Rhea maps this final reductase reaction to RHEA:73943.
