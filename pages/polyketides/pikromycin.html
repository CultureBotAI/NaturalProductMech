Biosynthesis curated

Step 1: DesVII (UniProtKB:Q9ZGH7, AAC68677.1, AF079762.1:1475..2755, EC 2.4.1.277 / EC 2.4.1.278) functions in an obligate (alpha-beta)3 complex with the auxiliary protein DesVIII (UniProtKB:Q9ZGH8, AAC68676.1, AF079762.1:119..1327) to transfer the D-desosamine moiety from dTDP-alpha-D-desosamine (CHEBI:27948) onto the 14-membered macrolactone aglycone narbonolide (CHEBI:29650) to form narbomycin (CHEBI:29649), the immediate substrate of PikC.

Step 2: PikC hydroxylates the 14-membered macrolide narbomycin to form pikromycin. RHEA:39887 grounds the same monooxygenase reaction using the corresponding protonated ChEBI species.
