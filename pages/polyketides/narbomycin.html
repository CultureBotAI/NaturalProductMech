PROPOSED marks an addition awaiting review; REVIEWED records curator sign-off on identity, structure, filing class and producer claims; DEPRECATED retains a superseded or out-of-scope record. The grounding note retains any unresolved alternatives. These tags do not establish the strength of producer or activity evidence.

A macrolide antibiotic that is narbonolide having a 3,4,6-trideoxy-3-(dimethylamino)-β-D-xylo-hexopyranosyl residue attached at position 6. It is biosynthesised by Streptomyces venezuelae.

1 DesVII macrolide glycosyltransferase
UniProtKB:Q9ZGH7		 CHEBI:29650 → CHEBI:29649 DOI:10.1021/ja049967j

Step 1: DesVII (UniProtKB:Q9ZGH7, AAC68677.1, AF079762.1:1475..2755, EC 2.4.1.277 / EC 2.4.1.278) functions in an obligate (alpha-beta)3 complex with the auxiliary protein DesVIII (UniProtKB:Q9ZGH8, AAC68676.1, AF079762.1:119..1327) to transfer the D-desosamine moiety from dTDP-alpha-D-desosamine (CHEBI:27948) onto the 14-membered macrolactone aglycone narbonolide (CHEBI:29650) to form narbomycin (CHEBI:29649).
