Step 1: This curates the Aspergillus nidulans FGSC A4 non-reducing polyketide synthase OrsA from the BGC0000057/AACD01000135.1 route; OrsA condenses acetyl-CoA with three malonyl-CoA units to form o-orsellinate, the branch precursor for F-9775 A biosynthesis.

Step 2: This curates the genetically supported OrsB/OrsC branch that diverts the OrsA orsellinic acid product toward F-9775 A and B. The exact isolated substrate, intermediates and individual OrsB and OrsC reactions remain unresolved, so this two-protein step is marked proposed and leaves enzyme_id empty.
