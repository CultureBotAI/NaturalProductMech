Mechanism & Biosynthesis Overview

Causal graph — biosynthesis 22 nodes, 20 edges

Partial genetic-perturbation view; mediation and chemical reaction steps are intentionally omitted. The 2009 alcAp-afoA experiments used an nkuA-deletion/stcJ-deletion background; AfoA was induced, not deleted. The 2010 ScpR experiments are abstract-bounded, including afoA deletion during ScpR induction. Shared outcome nodes denote readout categories, not pooled cultures. FGSC A4 accessions are native references, not verified experimental alleles. Claims concern asperfuranone at this owner's stereo-unspecified resolution, not every possible stereoisomer. No direct promoter binding, transport substrate, catalytic order or universal phenotype is asserted. The 2009 Results, Table 1, captions and partial Methods were inspected; figure images, detailed spectra and supplement were not. No primary isomer-level identity sign-off is claimed. The added2010 negative-control condition combines inpB deletion with alcAp-scpR induction and retains asperfuranone detection. It does not establish universal InpB dispensability, a quantitative yield, or direct ScpR promoter binding.
