Step 1: FAD- and NADPH-dependent N5-hydroxylation of L-ornithine to N5-hydroxy-L-ornithine by Sid1 (Ustilago maydis) or Sib2 (Schizosaccharomyces pombe), followed by acetyl-CoA-dependent N5-acetylation (Sib3) to N5-acetyl-N5-hydroxy-L-ornithine.

Step 2: Iterative assembly and macrocyclization of three glycine units and three N5-acetyl-N5-hydroxy-L-ornithine units by the multimodular NRPS Sid2 (Ustilago maydis) or Sib1 (Schizosaccharomyces pombe) to release deferrichrome.

Mechanism & Biosynthesis Overview

Causal graph — bioactivity 11 nodes, 10 edges

Fungal NRPS-directed biosynthesis of the cyclic hexapeptide trihydroxamate siderophore deferrichrome via Sid1/Sid2 (Ustilago maydis) and Sib1/Sib2/Sib3 (Schizosaccharomyces pombe), octahedral chelation of Fe(III) and CMM cations Ga(III) and Al(III), and xenosiderophore uptake via bacterial FhuA.
