A tetracarboxylic acid obtained by protonation of the four carboxylate groups of achromobactin. A siderophore biosynthesized by prokaryotes.

Biosynthesis curated

StepEnzymeReactionSubstrate → productEvidence
1 AcsD (NIS synthetase) and AcsE (decarboxylase) CHEBI:30769 + CHEBI:17115 → ungrounded PMID:19182782
2 AcsA and AcsC (NIS siderophore synthetases) CHEBI:30915 → CHEBI:65352 PMID:19482931

Step 1: ATP-dependent enantioselective condensation of citric acid with L-serine by AcsD followed by PLP-dependent decarboxylation by AcsE to O-citryl-ethanolamine.

Step 2: Amide condensation of O-citryl-ethanolamine with L-2,4-diaminobutyrate and two equivalents of 2-oxoglutaric acid to install the two 4-carboxy-4-oxobutanoyl chelating arms of achromobactin.
