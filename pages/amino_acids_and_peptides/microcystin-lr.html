microcystin-LR

CHEBI:6925 EXACT SEEDED cytotoxic

A microcystin consisting of D-alanyl, L-leucyl, (3S)-3-methyl-D-β-aspartyl,L-arginyl, 2S,3S,4E,6E,8S,9S)-3-amino-4,5,6,7-tetradehydro-9-methoxy-2,6,8-trimethyl-10-phenyldecanoyl, D-γ-glutamyl, and 2,3-didehydro-N-methylalanyl residues joined into a 25-membered macrocycle. Produced by the cyanobacterium Microcystis aeruginosa, it is the most studied of the microcystins.
