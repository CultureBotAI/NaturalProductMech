Step 1: Conversion of chorismic acid to salicylic acid by MbtI and ATP-dependent adenylation to salicyl-AMP by MbtA.

Step 2: Hybrid NRPS-PKS assembly of the 2-hydroxyphenyloxazoline-lysine-3-hydroxybutyrate-cyclolysine core from salicylic acid, L-serine, and L-lysine, followed by N6-hydroxylation and acylation with a methyl-esterified dicarboxylic acid chain to yield desferriexochelin 772MS.
