What these status tags mean

Curation status describes a workflow state. SEEDED means the record entered through the source-seeding workflow; it does not imply that no later curation occurred. PROPOSED marks an addition awaiting review; REVIEWED records curator sign-off on identity, structure, filing class and producer claims; DEPRECATED retains a superseded or out-of-scope record. These are workflow decisions, not independently verified human review. Agent-assisted contributions may appear in the record history.

Grounding describes the identifier mapping: EXACT uses an ontology identity asserted by the source; MINTED keeps a local identifier when no defensible ontology identity is available; REVIEW_NEEDED flags an exact structure whose ontology identity needs adjudication. The grounding note retains any unresolved alternatives. These tags do not establish the strength of producer or activity evidence.

Step 1: GtfB/tcp23 transfers N-acetylglucosamine from UDP-N-acetyl-alpha-D-glucosamine to Hpg4 of the teicoplanin aglycone. That Hpg4 glycosylation precedes downstream deacetylation, acylation and tailoring toward teicoplanin A2-4, so the immediate pseudoaglycone product is left ungrounded.

Step 2: GtfA/tcp8 transfers N-acetylglucosamine from UDP-N-acetyl-alpha-D-glucosamine to Bht6/3-Cl-6betaHty of a GtfB-primed teicoplanin pseudoaglycone. GtfA can also glycosylate the unmodified teicoplanin aglycone less efficiently, but Bht6 glycosylation is ordered here after Hpg4 glycosylation toward teicoplanin A2-4, so the immediate bis-glycosylated product is left ungrounded.

Step 3: Orf2*/tcp14 deacetylates the Hpg4 N-acetylglucosaminyl group of the teicoplanin pseudoaglycone, exposing the Hpg4 glucosamine for downstream N-acylation toward teicoplanin A2-4.

Step 4: Tcp24/tAtf transfers a fatty acyl group from an acyl-CoA donor to the Orf2*-exposed Hpg4 glucosamine of the teicoplanin pseudoaglycone. The immediate N-acylated bis-glycosylated product is left ungrounded because the record-specific A2 lipid donor is not grounded here.
