Step 1: Penicillium chrysogenum PcbAB condenses L-2-aminoadipate, L-cysteine, and L-valine to the ACV tripeptide. RHEA:23196 also consumes three ATP and water and releases three AMP, three diphosphates, and three H(+).

Step 2: Penicillium rubens/Penicillium chrysogenum Wisconsin 54-1255 IpnA/PcbC oxidatively cyclizes ACV to isopenicillin N. RHEA:22428 also consumes O2 and releases water; its product participant is CHEBI:58399 isopenicillin N(1-), while the sibling isopenicillin N record is grounded to neutral CHEBI:18165 with the same stereochemistry (and this ACV record is grounded to CHEBI:58572).
