3115 natural product structures, one record per Standard InChIKey, harmonized from 9 adopted sources.

A producer is not an occurrence. This corpus keeps the two apart because most sources do not. 3407 producer claims say a taxon makes the compound, and only 805 of those rest on evidence that addressed the organism. 2342 records carry a cited occurrence, which says only that someone found the compound there. Nothing promotes one to the other.
