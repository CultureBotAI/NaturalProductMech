NaturalProductMech

3116 natural product structures, one record per Standard InChIKey, harmonized from 10 adopted sources.

A producer is not an occurrence. This corpus keeps the two apart because most sources do not. 3407 producer claims say a taxon makes the compound, and only 806 of those rest on evidence that addressed the organism. 2341 records carry a cited occurrence, which says only that someone found the compound there. Nothing promotes one to the other.

How records are produced

The corpus reproduces offline from committed inventories: MIBiG (structures, producers, gene clusters), ChEBI (grounding, origins), LOTUS and CyanoMetDB (cited occurrences), NCBI Taxonomy (name resolution), NPClassifier (the filing pathway), PubChem BioAssay and BindingDB (measured activities and targets), and AntibioticMech (antimicrobial classification and cross-corpus links). Every record remains SEEDED until a curator signs off identity, structure, filing class, and producer claims; partial biosynthetic_pathway and causal_graphs curation is now underway.

— the repository's README

Each record states its curation status, and the schema defines what each one means. These are workflow decisions, not independently verified human review.

Every change to a record appends a curation event that names its curator; a record page lists its own under Curation history. Across the corpus, 8022 events name these curators: seed_from_sources (7284), codex (611), antigravity (67), Codex (39), OpenAI Codex (14), claude (5), codex/add-proteins-to-graphs (1), codex:add-proteins-to-graphs (1). 736 of the events are flagged llm_assisted.
