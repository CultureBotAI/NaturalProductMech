NaturalProductMech

3116 natural product structures, one record per Standard InChIKey, harmonized from 10 adopted sources.

A producer is not an occurrence. This corpus keeps the two apart because most sources do not. 3407 producer claims say a taxon makes the compound, and only 806 of those rest on evidence that addressed the organism. 2341 records carry a cited occurrence, which says only that someone found the compound there. Nothing promotes one to the other.
