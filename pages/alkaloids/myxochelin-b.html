Step 1: MxcE adenylates 2,3-dihydroxybenzoic acid for transfer to the MxcF aryl carrier protein; the carrier-protein thioester is left ungrounded as a pathway intermediate.

Step 2: MxcF supplies the aryl carrier protein that receives activated 2,3-DHBA from MxcE and presents it to MxcG; its isochorismatase module contributes to DHBA formation from chorismate.

Step 3: MxcG adenylates lysine, condenses two MxcF-delivered DHBA groups with lysine, and uses its reductase domain for two-electron release of the carrier-protein-bound thioester to a myxochelin aldehyde branch-point intermediate.

Step 4: MxcL transaminates the free myxochelin aldehyde intermediate to the primary amine branch product myxochelin B.
