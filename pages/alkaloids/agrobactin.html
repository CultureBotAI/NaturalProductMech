PROPOSED marks an addition awaiting review; REVIEWED records curator sign-off on identity, structure, filing class and producer claims; DEPRECATED retains a superseded or out-of-scope record. Step 1: AgbC is the AB083344.2 isochorismate synthase candidate; the reaction class is assigned from the UniProtKB/ARBA prediction rather than from a purified AgbC assay.

Step 2: AgbB is the AB083344.2 isochorismatase candidate required for catechol-compound production under iron limitation; the exact Rhea isochorismatase reaction is a UniProtKB/ARBA prediction.

Step 3: AgbA is the AB083344.2 2,3-dihydro-2,3-dihydroxybenzoate dehydrogenase candidate; the reaction class is assigned from the UniProtKB/ARBA prediction rather than from a purified AgbA assay.

Step 4: AgbD is the AB083344.2 phosphopantetheinyl-transferase candidate that would activate the aryl/peptidyl carrier proteins for 2,3-DHBA transfer and NRPS assembly.

Step 5: AgbE is the AB083344.2 2,3-dihydroxybenzoate-AMP ligase candidate that likely activates 2,3-DHBA for aryl-carrier-protein transfer.

Step 6: AgbF is the AB083344.2 probable NRPS candidate. The complete route from 2,3-DHBA to the catecholate siderophore agrobactin is left unresolved because individual thioester intermediates and the terminal product-forming steps were not resolved in the inspected sources.
