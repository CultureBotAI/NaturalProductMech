A bromoindole that is 2,3,5-tribromo-1H-indole which is substituted by a 5,7-dibromo-3-cyano-1H-indol-2-yl group at position 1. It is a biindole alkaloid isolated from the cyanobacterium Aetokthonos hydrillicola and causes vacuolar myelinopathy in bald eagles.

Step 1: AetF carries the first brominated tryptophan branch point in the convergent AETX pathway and converts L-tryptophan to 5-bromo-L-tryptophan.

Step 2: AetF performs the second tryptophan bromination to form the 5,7-dibromo-L-tryptophan substrate for AetD.

Step 3: AetE diverts 5-bromo-L-tryptophan into the tribromoindole arm by cleaving it to 5-bromoindole.

Step 4: AetA catalyzes the first pyrrole-ring bromination of 5-bromoindole to generate 3,5-dibromoindole.

Step 5: AetA carries out the second pyrrole-ring bromination, converting 3,5-dibromoindole to the AetB substrate 2,3,5-tribromoindole.

Step 6: AetD forms the 5,7-dibromo-indole-3-carbonitrile arm from 5,7-dibromo-L-tryptophan without a confirmed role for AetC.

Step 7: AetB completes AETX biosynthesis by coupling the separately assembled 5,7-dibromo-indole-3-carbonitrile and 2,3,5-tribromoindole monomers.
