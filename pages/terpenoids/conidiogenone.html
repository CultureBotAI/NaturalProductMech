Step 1: The C-terminal prenyltransferase domain of PchDS condenses isopentenyl diphosphate with (2E,6E)-farnesyl diphosphate to form (2E,6E,10E)-geranylgeranyl diphosphate in the conidiogenone pathway.

Step 2: The N-terminal terpene cyclase domain of PchDS cyclizes (2E,6E,10E)-geranylgeranyl diphosphate to deoxyconidiogenol.

Step 3: PchP450 is the Pc20g10870 P450 homolog proposed to catalyze the deoxyconidiogenol-to-conidiogenone oxidation in the Penicillium rubens AM920435.1 locus; the exact two-round oxidation was assayed with the Penicillium roqueforti PrP450 homolog, not directly with PchP450.
