Step 10: Streptomyces arenae PntE catalyzes the flavin-dependent Baeyer-Villiger oxidation of 1-deoxy-11-oxopentalenate to pentalenolactone D; PntD-catalyzed formation of pentalenolactone F is represented separately.

Step 20: Streptomyces arenae PntD catalyzes 2-oxoglutarate- and Fe(2+)-dependent oxidation of pentalenolactone D to pentalenolactone F; RHEA:34579 grounds the full net reaction to the pH 7.3 pentalenolactone F conjugate base with two 2-oxoglutarate/O2 equivalents per pentalenolactone F.

Step 30: Streptomyces arenae PntM/CYP161C2 catalyzes the terminal oxidative rearrangement of pentalenolactone F to pentalenolactone; upstream PntE and PntD steps are represented separately.
