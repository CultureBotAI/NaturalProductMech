Step 1: CotB2 cyclizes geranylgeranyl diphosphate to cyclooctat-9-en-7-ol, the first committed diterpene cyclization in cyclooctatin biosynthesis.

Step 2: CotB3 performs the first P450 hydroxylation downstream of CotB2, converting cyclooctat-9-en-7-ol to cyclooctat-9-en-5,7-diol.

Step 3: CotB4 performs the terminal C-18 P450 hydroxylation from cyclooctat-9-en-5,7-diol to cyclooctatin. CHEBI:78370 is the stereocomplete Rhea product; the NaturalProductRecord remains grounded to the partial-stereo MIBiG structure pending a record-level stereochemistry review.
