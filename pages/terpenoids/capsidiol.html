capsidiol

CHEBI:28283 EXACT SEEDED antifungal

An eremophilane sesquiterpenoid that is (+)-5-epi-aristolochene bearing additional 1β- and 3α-hydroxy substituents. It is a phytoalexin produced in Nicotiana and Capsicum plant species in response to pathogen attack.
