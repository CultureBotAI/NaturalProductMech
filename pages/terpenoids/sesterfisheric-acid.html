Step 1: NfSS cyclizes (2E,6E,10E,14E)-geranylfarnesyl diphosphate to the tetracyclic sesterterpene alcohol sesterfisherol. This single step records the sesterfisherol-forming RHEA:54068 reaction and leaves the downstream A1DN29/NfP450 oxidation to sesterfisheric acid unexpanded.

Step 2: Aspergillus fischeri (Neosartorya fischeri) NRRL 181 cytochrome P450 monooxygenase NfP450 (NFIA_055490 / EAW16200.1) oxidizes sesterfisherol (CHEBI:138046) to sesterfisheric acid (naturalproductmech:mibig-90c066a9a2).
