1 AlbAB cyclodipeptide oxidase RHEA:35531 CHEBI:71608 → CHEBI:71609 DOI:10.1038/s41467-024-48030-9

Step 1: AlbAB catalyzes two sequential alpha,beta-dehydrogenations of cyclo(L-phenylalanyl-L-leucyl) with O2 as the electron acceptor to form albonoursin and H2O2. This partial Streptomyces noursei ATCC 11455 pathway records the characterized CDO tailoring step and leaves AlbC-dependent cyclodipeptide precursor formation unexpanded.
