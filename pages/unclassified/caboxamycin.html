Mechanism & Biosynthesis Overview

Causal graph — biosynthesis 9 nodes, 9 edges

Five versioned native protein references are linked by explicitly inferred CDS-to-insert mappings to the reported pSET-cbxABCDE construct. Separate primary experimental edges describe caboxamycin production in S. albus J1074 and B29. The five complete native CDSs do not establish one-to-one cbx aliases, individual enzyme reactions or a physical protein complex; 15 nt of the next CDS is not a sixth intact protein. The authors report PCR sequence checking, but the exact assayed insert sequence was not independently inspected. The product endpoint is acid caboxamycin (compound 1), not its methyl ester or other derivatives. Native producer/BGC evidence grades and the natural-origin classification are unchanged.
