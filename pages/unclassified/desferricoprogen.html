A member of the class of 2,5-diketopiperazines that is 2,5-diketopiperazine which is substituted at positions 3 and 6 by 3-(hydroxyamino)propyl groups in which the nitrogens have been acylated by (2E)-5-hydroxy-3-methylpent-2-enoyl groups. The substituent at position 3 has been further modified by having its terminal hydroxy group esterified by condensation with the carboxy group of N2-acetyl-N5-hydroxy-L-ornithine in which the N5 nitrogen has been acylated by a (2E)-5-hydroxy-3-methylpent-2-enoyl group.

2 Coprogen nonribosomal peptide synthetase (SidD) and N-acetyltransferase CHEBI:79095 + CHEBI:15351 + CHEBI:15422 → CHEBI:133861 + CHEBI:83125 PMID:25062661

Step 1: N5-hydroxylation of L-ornithine to N5-hydroxy-L-ornithine followed by N5-acylation with mevalonate-derived trans-anhydromevalonyl-CoA to yield N5-trans-anhydromevalonyl-N5-hydroxy-L-ornithine.

Step 2: NRPS-catalyzed condensation of three N5-trans-anhydromevalonyl-N5-hydroxy-L-ornithine units (forming a diketopiperazine ring linked via an ester bond to a third monomer) producing desferricoprogen B, followed by Nalpha-acetylation to yield desferricoprogen.

Mechanism & Biosynthesis Overview

Fungal NRPS-directed biosynthesis of the ester-linked diketopiperazine trihydroxamate siderophore desferricoprogen in Neurospora crassa and Aspergillus niger, hexadentate chelation of Fe(III), CMM cations Ga(III), Al(III), In(III), Cr(III), and Ni(II), plus Cu(II), Zn(II), and Cd(II), and bacterial xenosiderophore uptake via FhuE.
