Step 1: SCO3229/HmaS is curated as the 4-hydroxymandelate synthase that supplies the L-4-hydroxyphenylglycine branch of CDA4a biosynthesis. HmaS sits at the first step of the pathway from 4-hydroxyphenylpyruvate to 4-hydroxyphenylglycine; the exact SCO3229 4-hydroxyphenylpyruvate to (S)-4-hydroxymandelate reaction is inferred from the HmaS assignment and therefore kept proposed.
