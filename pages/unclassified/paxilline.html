paxilline

CHEBI:34907 EXACT SEEDED

An indole diterpene alkaloid with formula C27H33NO4 isolated from Penicillium paxilli. It is a potent inhibitor of large conductance Ca2+- and voltage-activated K+ (BK)-type channels.
