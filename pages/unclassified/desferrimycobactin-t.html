Step 1: Conversion of chorismate to salicylic acid by MbtI and ATP-dependent adenylation to salicyl-AMP by MbtA for loading onto the aryl carrier domain of MbtB.

Step 2: Hybrid NRPS-PKS assembly of the phenyloxazoline-peptidyl-polyketide core and N6-hydroxylation/acylation of L-lysine residues to yield cell-envelope-associated lipophilic desferrimycobactin T.
