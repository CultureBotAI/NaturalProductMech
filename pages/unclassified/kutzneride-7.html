Step 1: KtzQ catalyzes the first tandem halogenase step in the kutzneride pathway, forming 7-chloro-L-tryptophan from free L-tryptophan before KtzR installs the 6-chlorine in a separate downstream step.

Step 2: KtzR catalyzes the second tandem halogenase step in the kutzneride pathway, installing the 6-chlorine on 7-chloro-L-tryptophan to form 6,7-dichloro-L-tryptophan.
