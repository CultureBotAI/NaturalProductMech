Step 1: N5-hydroxylation of L-ornithine to generate N(5)-hydroxy-L-ornithine.

Step 2: Acetyl-CoA-dependent N5-acetylation of N(5)-hydroxy-L-ornithine followed by ATP-dependent NRPS activation and head-to-tail cyclodimerization of two N5-acetyl-N5-hydroxy-L-ornithine units to form the 2,5-diketopiperazine dihydroxamate siderophore rhodotorulic acid.

Mechanism & Biosynthesis Overview

Causal graph — bioactivity 11 nodes, 10 edges

Biosynthesis of the diketopiperazine dihydroxamate siderophore rhodotorulic acid in basidiomycetous yeasts (Rhodotorula spp.), formation of binuclear 3:2 complexes with Fe(III) and CMM trivalent cations Ga(III), Al(III), and Cr(III), and bacterial xenosiderophore uptake via Escherichia coli FhuE.
