Mechanism & Biosynthesis Overview

Causal graph — biosynthesis 5 nodes, 4 edges

Partial precursor chemistry for everninomicin D: purified, N-terminal His6-tagged EvdS6 expressed from pET28a(+) in E. coli Tuner(DE3) was assayed with UDP-GlcA. UDP-4-keto-D-xylose and UDP-D-xylose are candidate precursors of the H- and G-rings, respectively. Their downstream incorporation, the native UDP-versus-TDP donor preference, and complete everninomicin D biosynthesis were not demonstrated.
