Step 1: DesIV (AAC68681.1, AF079762.1:5661..6674, EC 4.2.1.46) catalyzes NAD+-dependent 4,6-dehydration of dTDP-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:15700) to dTDP-4-dehydro-6-deoxy-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:16128) in the Streptomyces venezuelae ATCC 15439 dTDP-D-desosamine pathway. RHEA:17221 represents the same reaction using the anionic species CHEBI:57477 and CHEBI:57649.

Step 2: DesI (AAC68684.1, AF079762.1:8651..9898, EC 2.6.1.33) catalyzes the PLP-dependent C-4 transamination of dTDP-4-dehydro-6-deoxy-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:16128) to dTDP-4-amino-4,6-dideoxy-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:68523).

Step 3: DesII (AAC68683.1, AF079762.1:7140..8597, EC 4.3.1.30) is a radical SAM [4Fe-4S] ammonia-lyase that catalyzes the redox-neutral C-4 deamination of dTDP-4-amino-4,6-dideoxy-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:68523) to dTDP-3-dehydro-4,6-dideoxy-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:27645) and NH4+. RHEA:39647 represents the same reaction using the anionic species CHEBI:68501 and CHEBI:76280.

Step 4: DesV (AAC68680.1, AF079762.1:4502..5641, EC 2.6.1.106) catalyzes the PLP-dependent C-3 transamination of dTDP-3-dehydro-4,6-dideoxy-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:27645) with L-glutamate to form dTDP-3-amino-3,4,6-trideoxy-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:10502). RHEA:39907 represents the same reaction using the anionic species CHEBI:76280 and CHEBI:63262.

Step 5: DesVI (AAC68678.1, AF079762.1:2811..3524, EC 2.1.1.234) catalyzes the SAM-dependent C-3 N,N-dimethylation of dTDP-3-amino-3,4,6-trideoxy-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:10502) to form dTDP-alpha-D-desosamine (CHEBI:27948). This enzymatic pathway terminates at the nucleotide-activated donor dTDP-alpha-D-desosamine (CHEBI:27948; anionic Rhea species CHEBI:63260) rather than asserting an enzymatic release of free desosamine (CHEBI:32540).

Mechanism & Biosynthesis Overview

Causal graph — biosynthesis 11 nodes, 10 edges

Streptomyces venezuelae ATCC 15439 DesIV/DesI/DesII/DesV/DesVI enzymatic pathway from dTDP-alpha-D-glucose (CHEBI:15700) to the nucleotide-activated donor dTDP-alpha-D-desosamine (CHEBI:27948). Free desosamine (CHEBI:32540) is the deoxysugar moiety transferred from dTDP-alpha-D-desosamine onto macrolactone aglycones by DesVII/DesVIII.
