oxetanocin A

CHEBI:86012 EXACT SEEDED antibacterial

A nucleoside analogue found in Bacillus megaterium in which an adenine moiety is attached to position 2 of a of an oxetane ring which is substituted at positions 3 and 4 by hydroxymethyl groups.
