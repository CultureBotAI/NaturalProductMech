# Myxochromide Review Evidence

PR #923 preserves the two original partial exact-owner reviews and their
historical evidence. `manifest.json` maps original paths to deterministic gzip
artifacts with raw and stored hashes. Source snapshots are pinned to the
review's Git base, not the subsequently curated record.

Publisher PDFs, extracted text, figures, publication abstract metadata and
denial payloads are reference-only with URLs and hashes; they are not
redistributed. The initial failed strict command never produced its planned
TSV. Its receipt and the successful native retry are retained separately.
Runtime installations are explicitly distinguished from evidence artifacts.

The checkpoint covers all 3,115 owners and 243 graph components: 36 immutable
observations, 25 assessed owners (one historical completed assessment and 24
partial assessments), and 3,090 unassessed owners. It is a historical snapshot,
not a declaration that the campaign is complete. All native records remain
SEEDED. The 206 access observations are historical observations, not a count
of currently inaccessible sources.

Issues #924 and #925 retain unresolved identity questions. A qualification
does not correct or experimentally establish either chemical structure.
