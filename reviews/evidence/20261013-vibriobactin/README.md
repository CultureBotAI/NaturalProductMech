# Vibriobactin Review Evidence

`manifest.json` maps the original temporary paths in the two immutable reviews
to compressed, checksum-pinned artifacts. Decompress with `gzip -dc`; SHA-256
and byte lengths are recorded for both original and stored bytes. Archived
helpers are provenance, not commands to replay: several write exclusively to
their original workspace.

The archive includes successful and failed checks, exact chemistry and protein
audits, public structured source snapshots, the complete 3,115-owner campaign
checkpoint, its predecessor, and the inherited source-access stop ledger.
Native protein deposits remain historical pins. No publisher full text is
included. Runtime/cache directories are explicitly distinguished from evidence.

The checkpoint predates PR #894 remediation. It records 12 partially reviewed
owners, 15 observations, zero completed scientific reviews and 3,103 unassessed
owners. It is historical, not a claim that corrected files still have old hashes.
The immutable reviews remain unchanged; later scoped findings require successors.
