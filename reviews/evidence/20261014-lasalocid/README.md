not molecular identity or scientific status. Initial observations are under
`reviews/structured/20261014T041547Z-lasalocid-2/` and
`reviews/structured/20261014T041554Z-lasalocid/`.

`manifest.json` pins the retained public database responses and pre-change

## Primary Sources

- DOI:10.1271/bbb.80631, PMID:19129623: the inspected advance-view PDF methods
  and deposition statement name ATCC35851 and AB449340/pKSL. Native gene
  inactivation attempts were unsuccessful. Purified Lsd19 biochemistry is a
  separate evidence route. The PDF receipt and content hash are retained, but
  the full copyrighted paper is not redistributed. Its pages 2 and 3 were also
  visually inspected. The GenBank AB449340.1 source instead names ATCC31180;
  neither attribution is declared correct by this review.
- [Wang et al., 2011](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/AEM.02915-10):
  chemical-identification results report FXJ1.172 product as lasalocid A using
  590-Da mass, 304-nm UV maximum and MS2 comparison with JCM3373. This supports
  strain-level production within the reported analytical resolution, not a
  new absolute-stereochemical determination or full KT591188 characterization.

KT591188.1 identifies FXJ1.172 and describes a putative partial cluster.
Accordingly, the producer and locus citations are routed separately; all
original source references remain in the raw inventory and correction notes.
NCBITaxon:1931 is not silently changed to the separate FXJ1.172 node 710705.

## Limits

The strain disagreement, exact biological salt processing, original absolute
configuration and complete primary locus attribution remain unresolved.
Some primary articles were abstract-only or inaccessible; access-stop details
are retained in `web-observations.json`. No access-control bypass was attempted.
Source-faithful `SOURCE_ASSERTION` / `CLUSTER_UNSTATED` grades and `SEEDED`
status remain. Tests establish representation and reproducibility, not biology.
