# Indigoidine Review Evidence

This archive accompanies PR #874 and the two immutable indigoidine reviews
`20261013T064409Z-indigoidine-2` and `20261013T064413Z-indigoidine`.
`manifest.json` maps historical temporary paths to byte-preserved artifacts,
with SHA-256 hashes and sizes. Large sources use lossless gzip storage, with
both original and stored hashes. Historical scripts are inert `.py.txt` files;
several create exclusive outputs and must not be replayed over their workspace.

The original campaign checkpoint retains 3,115 owners, 242 components, seven
partial reviews, zero completed reviews and 3,108 unassessed owners. Its
`no_github_mutations` describes the review session, not this later publication.
All corpus and review bytes remain unchanged. Previous evidence archives and
historical Git inputs remain available at their recorded revisions.

The archive preserves chemistry/source audits, native sequence records,
validation receipts, the diagnostic queue and inherited access stops. Official
NCBI sequence records and UniProt data remain attributed through their original
source URLs, identifiers and retrieval metadata. Database sequence agreement
does not establish an experimental construct or live source freshness.

Five primary-source artifacts are deliberately **reference-only**: the J1074
author PDF, CCM3239 publisher HTML, supplement and figure, and FRI-5 PubMed XML.
Their citation/URL, observed byte count and SHA-256 are retained, but full text
and images are not republished because redistribution rights were not
established. Fresh-checkout tests cannot verify those omitted bytes or replay
the literature review. Source accessibility is not a redistribution license;
neither this archive nor its tests resolves that limitation. No stopped source
was retrieved again for publication.

Publication finding [#875](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/NaturalProductMech/issues/875)
is addressed by the archive, explicit omissions and fresh-checkout integrity
tests. Scientific finding
[#876](https://github.com/CultureBotAI/NaturalProductMech/issues/876) remains
open: BGC0002295 compound 2 uses a constitution inconsistent with the indigoidine
label, and an evidence-backed collision-aware correction is not established.
Required primary/current-source checks and the pinned RDKit calculation remain
incomplete. Neither review is a scientific pass or status promotion.
